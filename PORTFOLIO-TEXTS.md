## 1. Landing Page – Local Dental Clinic (concept)
Folder: `landing-page/`

**Title:** Conversion-Focused Landing Page for a Local Clinic (Concept)

**Description:**
Concept landing page for a local service business. Goal: turn visitors into booked appointments.
- Clear value proposition and call-to-action above the fold
- Services, social proof, FAQ and a booking form
- Fully responsive (mobile-first), fast: single file, no heavy libraries
- Accessible: semantic HTML, form labels, keyboard-friendly
Delivered in 24–48 hours. Can be adapted to any niche (salon, law firm, repair, fitness).

**Skills:** Landing Page, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Responsive Design

## 2. AI Support & Booking Chatbot (demo)
Folder: `ai-chatbot/`

**Title:** Support & Appointment Booking Chatbot

**Description:**
Website chatbot that answers common questions, books appointments step by step and captures leads.
- FAQ answers (hours, pricing, location) with quick-reply buttons
- Guided booking flow with email validation
- Live lead capture panel and human hand-off
- Demo runs rule-based; production version connects to OpenAI / Claude trained on the client's own
  documents and deploys to website, Telegram or WhatsApp
Reduces repetitive support questions and captures leads 24/7.

**Skills:** Chatbot Development, OpenAI API, JavaScript, Conversational UI

## 3. Lead Automation Dashboard (demo)
Folder: `lead-automation/`

**Title:** Lead Capture, Scoring & Reporting Automation

**Description:**
Automated workflow: web form → validation → lead scoring (Hot/Warm/Cold) → storage → team alert.
- Instant scoring based on budget, company size and urgency
- Live KPIs: total leads, hot leads, pipeline value, hot rate
- Search, filter and one-click CSV export
- Production version connects to Google Sheets, HubSpot, Slack or email via Zapier / Make / n8n
Eliminates manual copy-paste and ensures hot leads are never missed.

**Skills:** Automation, Zapier, Make, API Integration, JavaScript
