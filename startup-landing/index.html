Questions, answered

Does Orbit record my meetings?

It can transcribe audio to create notes, and you choose whether to keep or delete transcripts. Everything is optional and configurable.

Which meeting tools does it support?

Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams in beta, with more on the way.

Is my data private?

Your notes are yours. We never sell data or use it to train public models.
