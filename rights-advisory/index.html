How we work

A calm, step-by-step process

  1. 01

    Listen

    A private conversation to understand your situation and what you want to achieve.

  2. 02

    Assess

    We analyse the facts against applicable standards and explain what they mean.

  3. 03

    Plan

    You receive a written plan with clear options, timelines and next steps.

  4. 04

    Support

    We stay alongside you while you act, and adjust the plan as things change.

FAQ

Common questions

Is my enquiry confidential?

Yes. We only use what you share to respond to you, and we never pass it on without your consent.

Do you provide legal representation?

Our advisory work is not a substitute for legal representation. Where a lawyer is needed, we explain why and help you prepare to work with one.

How much does a consultation cost?

The first conversation is free. If further work is needed, we agree the scope and fees with you in writing before we start.

How quickly will you respond?

We reply within two working days. If your situation is urgent, tell us in the form and we will prioritise it.
